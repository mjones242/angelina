About Faith Forward

Faith Forward is a website created by Angelina Jones and Mckenna Roddick. Its goal is to inspire young kids to grow in faith using fun, engaging activities. Everything in this website is original. The website was coded entirely by Angelina in html and css code and all the art was done by hand by Mckenna. Every week it encourages users to check in to read that week's bible story along with an activity, and complete a weekly challenge related to faith and kindness. This project took about 5 weeks to complete and was originally made for the St Carlo Acutis challenge from The Diocese of St. Catharines. The website also has a section with prayers and a simple tutorial on how to pray the rosary, and it also has many tools that teachers can use in their classrooms such as the the Prayer Challenge Monthly Calendars, a tutorial showing you how to make your very own rosary, and lots of colouring pages! This website took a lot of hard work and dedication, including hours of coding, crafting, and many dried-out markers! We hope this inspires people to live out their faith and show kindess everywhere they go.
