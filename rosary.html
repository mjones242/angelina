The Rosary

About the Rosary

The rosary has been around for over 600 years. Praying the rosary is a way to become closer with God and is a form of meditation. It also brings us closer to Jesus and it can bring us peace and spiritual growth. Many saints encourage us to pray the rosary. For example, Mary has appeared in various forms urging us to pray the daily rosary, and Saint Carlo Acutis encourages us to pray the rosary every day, calling it a 'Powerful weapon to fight against evil and a means to grow closer to God'.
