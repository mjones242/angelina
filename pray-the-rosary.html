How To Pray The Rosary

1. Hold the crucifix and make the Sign of the Cross.

2. Pray the Apostles Creed.

3. Move to the first bead and pray the Our Father.

4. Pray one Hail Mary for each of the three beads, symbolizing faith, hope, and charity.

5. After the three Hail Marys, pray one Glory Be.

6. There are 5 decades in total, each consisting of 10 small beads.

For each decade:

  • Announce the Mystery.
  • Pray the Our Father
  • Pray ten Hail Marys, one for each bead, while meditating on the Mystery.
  • Pray the Glory Be and Oh My Jesus

7. Repeat step 6 for each of the decades.

8. After all 5 decades, hold the centerpiece and pray the Hail Holy Queen

9. Finish with the Final Prayer and the Sign Of The Cross
